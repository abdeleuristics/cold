The problem

You're Not Competing on Price. You're Competing for Visibility.

Here's what actually happens most months. You put money into Google Ads. Some of it turns into clicks. A few of those clicks turn into calls. Most of those calls were never going to hire you: wrong service area, wrong budget, wrong person on the other end of the phone.

By the time you've paid for all of it, the math rarely works out the way the ad platform promised it would.

Meanwhile, the facility manager two buildings over from your last job is actively looking for a new cleaning company right now. They're not searching Google. They're asking around, taking calls, and hiring whoever gets in front of them first with something that looks credible.

That's the entire game. Not who has the best price. Who gets in the room.

The shift

There's a Simpler Way In: The Walkthrough.

A walkthrough is the moment a real decision-maker, the owner, the facility manager, the office manager, stands in the building with you and lets you see exactly what you'd be bidding on.

Everything upstream of that moment, the ad spend, the landing pages, the cold calls that go to voicemail, is just an attempt to manufacture it. Most of it fails before it gets there.

We skip the manufacturing process. We reach out directly to decision-makers inside the zip codes you actually serve, and we keep going until a walkthrough lands on your calendar. Not a click. Not a form fill. A real person, in a real building, ready to see your bid.

What "qualified" actually means

We Don't Send You Meetings. We Send You Decision-Makers, Ready to Buy.

Every lead-gen company says their leads are "qualified." Almost none of them can tell you what that word actually means. Here's exactly what has to be true before a walkthrough ever lands on your calendar, and why each one protects you.

01 Inside your serviceable area

We only reach out inside the zip codes or radius you set during onboarding. You'll never get a walkthrough for a building three counties away that you'd lose money just driving to.

02 Exclusive to you

This lead is never sold or shared with another cleaning company. You're not bidding against three other crews we sent the same walkthrough to. If you're in the room, you're the only cleaning company we put there.

03 A real decision-maker

The contact is the owner, the facility manager, or the office manager, someone who can actually sign a contract. Not a receptionist collecting quotes for someone else's spreadsheet.

04 A real need

The prospect is actively looking for a cleaning service, or open to switching from whoever they have now. You're not walking into a building where the decision was already made six months ago.

05 Walkthrough attended and completed

The meeting actually happened. Someone showed up, walked the space with you, and had the conversation.

06 A good-faith agreement

If a prospect is wasting your time, you flag it as unqualified and we replace it. We're not going to argue with you about whether a walkthrough was worth your afternoon.

Six checks. Every walkthrough. That's the whole standard.

"If it doesn't meet that standard, it doesn't count. We reschedule it or replace it, at no charge."

If the prospect doesn't show up, we reschedule or replace the walkthrough at no charge. If you believe a walkthrough was unqualified, flag it within 48 hours with a short reason and we'll replace it. This isn't a courtesy. It's the only way this model works for either of us.
