Payment & Package Terms

  • Payment: 100% upfront.
  • Package validity: walkthroughs are delivered until your package is fully completed.

What Counts as a Qualified Walkthrough

Every walkthrough is checked against this standard before it counts toward your package.

  • Inside your serviceable area: within the zip codes or radius you set during onboarding.
  • Exclusive to you: this lead is never sold or shared with another cleaning company.
  • Decision-maker: the contact is the owner, facility manager, or office manager, someone who can sign a contract.
  • Real need: the prospect is actively looking for a cleaning service or open to switching from their current one.
  • Walkthrough attended & completed.
  • Good faith agreement: if the prospect is wasting your time, flag it as unqualified and we will replace it.

Unqualified & Replacement Policy

  • No-show: if the prospect doesn't attend, we reschedule or replace it at no charge.
  • Flag window: flag unqualified walkthroughs within 48 hours with a short reason.

What You Receive per Lead

Contact name, role, phone, email, business name, address, facility type or size, current provider (if known), walkthrough date and time, and the full email thread with the prospect, so you know exactly what was discussed before you call.

Best Practice

  • Call the lead within 5 minutes of it being sent to you.
  • Confirm the walkthrough the day before by text or call.
  • Show up on time and send your quote within 24–48 hours.
  • Update the lead status (quoted, won, lost) so we can improve targeting.

Your Responsibilities

  • Set your service area and ideal client profile during onboarding.
  • Have one point of contact.
  • Pick up the phone for forwarded leads.
