Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Analytikly LLC FZ ("Agentiklabs," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit this website, submit a form, use our chat widget, book a call, or otherwise interact with our services. By using this site, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in three ways:

  • Information you give us directly, such as your name, phone number, email address, company details, and any message you submit through our chat widget, contact form, or booking calendar.
  • Information collected automatically, such as your IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, and referral source, through standard web analytics and the visitor-identification tooling described in Section 4.
  • Communications data, such as the content and metadata of text messages or calls, if you opt in to receive them as described in Section 3.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule qualification calls;
  • Evaluate whether our services are a fit for your business;
  • Deliver, manage, and improve our lead-generation and appointment-setting services;
  • Send appointment confirmations, reminders, and service-related updates by email, phone, or text (only where you've consented to text, per Section 3);
  • Maintain the security and proper functioning of this website; and
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. SMS & Text Messaging Communications

If you submit your phone number through our chat widget or forms and check the applicable consent box, you agree to receive text messages from Agentiklabs related to your inquiry, scheduled appointments, or (only where a separate box is checked) promotional offers.

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase or service.

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for assistance. See our carrier and CTIA compliance terms for additional detail.

We do not share your phone number with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

This site uses cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences and understand how visitors use the site. We also use a website visitor-identification pixel (currently provided by Leadsy AI) that can associate site visits with business contact information available through that provider's data sources. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect how parts of this site function.

You can exercise these rights, opt out of text messages (Section 3), or opt out of marketing emails at any time by contacting us at a@agentiklabs.org.
