How it works

A simple model: at least 8 walkthroughs a month, priced per walkthrough

Step 01

We define your service area together

Before anything launches, we agree in writing on exactly which zip codes you serve and who counts as a decision-maker for your business: facility managers, property managers, office managers, or business owners. No ambiguity, no arguing later.

Step 02

We reach out directly, no ad spend

Targeting, outreach, follow-up, scheduling: all handled by us, straight to decision-makers in the zip codes you serve. Your team touches nothing until a walkthrough is on the calendar.

Step 03

You get at least 8 walkthroughs a month

Every walkthrough is tied to a real decision-maker in your service area, not a click, not an ad impression. If one doesn't meet the bar or doesn't show, we replace it.

Every walkthrough puts you face-to-face with a real decision-maker, in a building you could be cleaning.

FAQ

Questions cleaning companies ask us

How is this different from what I'm doing now to get clients?

Most ways cleaning companies grow are unpredictable and pull time away from running the business. We reach out directly to decision-makers, facility managers, property managers, office managers, and business owners, in the zip codes you actually serve, and only walkthroughs that meet your criteria land on your calendar.

Do you do residential?

No. We only work with commercial facilities.

Can I exclude certain types of businesses?

Yes. During onboarding you fill out a short form with the categories you want us to skip.

How does pricing work exactly?

Priced per walkthrough: we'll walk you through the exact numbers and how onboarding works when we talk, because the right structure depends on your service area and volume targets. What we can tell you now: there's no bloated monthly retainer, and you get at least 8 walkthroughs a month.

How fast will I see walkthroughs?

We'll give you an honest timeline based on your service area and market. What we won't do is hide behind a six-month "ramp-up period." Our model doesn't reward us for stalling.

What do you need from me?

About 20 minutes during onboarding to lock in your service area and decision-maker criteria, and a team ready to run the walkthroughs. We handle everything up to the walkthrough itself.

Why do you want to talk before starting?

Because we only get paid for walkthroughs, we can't afford to take on cleaning companies we can't fill calendars for. Reaching out first is us qualifying you as much as you qualifying us. That's exactly the standard we then apply to every walkthrough we book for you.
