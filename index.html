FAQ

Questions cleaning companies ask us

How is this different from running Google Ads?

Google Ads charges you per click whether or not that click is even a facility manager who needs a cleaning company. We reach out directly to decision-makers, facility managers, property managers, office managers, and business owners, in the zip codes you actually serve, and only walkthroughs land on your calendar. That single difference changes everything about how the work gets done.

What counts as a qualified walkthrough?

A walkthrough with a facility manager, property manager, office manager, or business owner, in the zip codes you actually serve, agreed in writing before launch. If a walkthrough doesn't meet that bar, or doesn't show up, we replace it.

How does pricing work exactly?

Priced per walkthrough: we'll walk you through the exact numbers and how onboarding works when we talk, because the right structure depends on your service area and volume targets. What we can tell you now: there's no bloated monthly retainer, and you get at least 20 walkthroughs a month.

How fast will I see walkthroughs?

We'll give you an honest timeline based on your service area and market. What we won't do is hide behind a six-month "ramp-up period." Our model doesn't reward us for stalling.

What do you need from me?

About 20 minutes during onboarding to lock in your service area and decision-maker criteria, and a team ready to run the walkthroughs. We handle everything up to the walkthrough itself.

Why do you want to talk before starting?

Because we only get paid for walkthroughs, we can't afford to take on cleaning companies we can't fill calendars for. Reaching out first is us qualifying you as much as you qualifying us. That's exactly the standard we then apply to every walkthrough we book for you.
